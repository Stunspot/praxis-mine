# Description

Praxis Mine evaluates outside AI skills, repositories, prompts, tools, and workflows before they enter a working stack. It compares the candidate with existing capabilities, identifies the mechanisms that provide a real improvement, checks rights and operational risks, and recommends whether to test, adapt, monitor, extract selectively, or reject it. The result is a bounded adoption decision rather than an enthusiastic bulk install.

The rediscovery cabinet keeps purpose, mechanism, applicability and project context beside native source links, revision history and chronological evaluations. Cabinet of Curiosities and Research Index offer distinct reading compositions over the same SQLite ledger; they do not reactivate dormant recall machinery.

# Usage Notes

Copy the .zip from "Additional Files" to your Codex, Claude Code, or other harness, or add the .zip to a project knowledge base in Chat. Attach or reference the .zip in chat and say, "Install this Augment." The prompt in this post is optional.

- Extract the kit and use its Open.cmd or Open.command launcher with Python 3.11+.
- Resolve your existing data home before creating a new store. Search by purpose or mechanism, record kind and native disposition; select a finding to read its provenance and evaluations.
- Create or revise native source/candidate records, record a gated evaluation, and export the ledger or project handoff. Saves check native revisions; reconcile stale edits before saving again.
- Reopen with the launcher after reboot. Shortcuts require consent, and Arm’s Reach stays parked. Native macOS execution remains unverified.

- Give it a link, local folder, or the relevant files, plus the outcome you hope the candidate will improve and the baseline you already use.
- It evaluates before adoption; it does not install or execute the candidate unless you separately ask and authorize that work.
- License, privacy, security, runtime, and dependency constraints matter. Popularity is a lead, not proof that something belongs in your stack.
