# Judge the useful difference

Start with the recommendation and its actual scope. A skip can need only a reason. A saved consequential assessment should let a later owner reconstruct the baseline, candidate revision, observed comparison, rights and remaining test. The native helper records that declared judgment; it cannot authenticate evidence references or judge whether the experiment was good.

## Use the native gate

Use assets/evaluation.template.json as the input shape. The seven benefit dimensions are new_outcome, executable_leverage, domain_judgment, evidence_quality, maintainability, portability and context_efficiency. The five risks are security, privacy, license, cost_dependency and overlap. Give each an explicit integer 0–5. Zero risk means none found within the stated inspection; uncertainty is not zero. Five represents severe or unresolved risk. The weighted score only ranks attention.

Choose preferred_disposition deliberately. Explicit reject and monitor cannot be promoted by arithmetic. Security/privacy/license scores 4–5 reject an actionable choice. A high-overlap pilot is held at monitor; quarry or adapt may still be appropriate when the evidence identifies a useful narrower mechanism. No number creates adoption authority.

For pilot, quarry or adapt, supply decision_context.baseline (the existing alternative), comparison (same-task evidence and limits), rights_basis (source and permitted use), reason (why this choice follows), and rights_status. Status is compatible, not_applicable, unresolved or incompatible. Compatible reuse also needs a recorded candidate license conclusion; LICENSE.md is a filename, not that conclusion. Use not_applicable only for genuinely independent ideas without copying protected material. Unresolved rights or missing basis holds an actionable choice at monitor; incompatible rights rejects it. These are declared facts: the model must actually inspect the relevant evidence and restrictions.

Add owner and action_scope when they are known and useful. Name the canonical destination and authorized work, not a speculative organization chart. Record next_test as the smallest comparison or next action; rejection can omit it and retain its reason without inventing a future test. Evidence references must identify what was examined, with notes explaining important negative or conflicting observations.

## Keep the assessment attached to its object

An evaluation binds material candidate fields and source locator/access posture in provenance. The room, report and handoff use only assessments matching those fields. A material refresh returns the projection to triage; the historical record remains visible. Cosmetic titles and project tags do not stale technical evidence. Evaluations recorded before snapshot binding are historical until reassessed; no migration fabricates missing provenance. Repeating identical input for the same material is idempotent; a new material revision receives a new evaluation identity.

Pilot means bounded trial, quarry means selective extraction, adapt means a native fit, monitor means a specific reason to wait, and reject means decline. None proves executed integration. When integration is authorized, the receiving capability owner continues from the source and evidence to the requested verified outcome.

## Situated judgment

An attractive dashboard that imports correctly but hides its start and return controls has not passed its user task. Keep its successful import as narrow evidence; fail the navigation criterion and repair or skip the interface. A parser that exposes malformed rows where the baseline silently drops them may justify adapting that isolated behavior after valid-row regression checks. A duplicate wrapper with no gain should be skipped without manufacturing a twelve-score research project. An inaccessible repository remains an uninspected lead; its marketing claims can justify a look later, not a capability claim now.
